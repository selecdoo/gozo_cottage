## 2. Texte der Originalseite (wörtlich, Auszug der relevanten Aussagen)

**Startseite:** „Gozo Cottage was born in Gozo ~ the isle of Calypso. The idyllic atmosphere, the myths and the culinary traditions gave rise to the typical Mediterrean gastronomy. Blessed with the sun, the fertile soil in terraced fields and the fresh groundwater agricultural products harvested in Gozo are well renowned for their superior taste and quality. Gozo Cottage was setup with the sole purpose to use and promote local Gozitan Produce. The company, through its young and vibrant management seeks to bring back to life and offer to its esteemed clientele the old traditional recipes in full respect of traditional methods and derived only and exclusively from fresh products grown and harvested within the Island Region of Gozo. Gozo Cottage guarantees that all its products are genuine products produced on the island of Gozo from fresh agricultural products expertly grown and harvested by the toil and dedication of Gozitan farmers.“

**Startseite – Diversification:** „By investing in infrastructure, plant and equipment the finished project aims at producing a range of different products including: Fresh and Aged Cheeses · A range of cured meats using local pork · Different types of Jam sourcing local raw materials (fruits) · A Winery, providing consumers locally produced wines, sparkling wines, dessert/sweet wines and liqueur wines as opposed to current imports. […] A Winery and Cellars · Meat Processing/aging area · Dairy Production area including raw milk reception area · Jam Processing Area · Packaging · Testing/Research/Tasting Room · Olive oil Production Area · Vinegar Production area · Ancillary facilities“

**About Us:** „All Gozo Cottage products share the same secret – the best agri-produce of Gozo, harvested and processed on the same day, no added artificial flavours, nor preservatives, nor dyes. The end results:- Genuine produce of Gozo.“ – Facilities: „modern facilities located in Gozo […] a mixture of traditional recipes and latest technologies […] The highest standard in hygiene is ensured by strict adherence to HACCP“ – Location: Maltese islands = Malta, Gozo, Comino; Gozo „quaintly attractive for its less industrial, rural way of life […] renowned for its fine agriculture produce thanks to the combination of sun, fresh water and proximity to the sea“ – Agro-Food: „a very wide range of traditional marmalades, honey, carob syrup, sun-dried tomatoes, handpicked capers, peppered cheeselets, olives and natural sea salt. Our products can be found in many selected delicatessens and in most of the leading establishments all over Malta and Gozo.“

**Process:** Olivenernte Ende September bis Mitte November, traditionell von Hand (Familien, Netze unter den Bäumen), Pressung innerhalb von 24 h, nur mechanisch; ca. 7–8 kg Oliven pro Liter Extra Virgin Olive Oil; ca. 2 h Extraktion. Sonnengetrocknete Tomaten: halbiert, mit Meersalz bestreut, in der Sommersonne getrocknet.

**News-Unterseiten** (`/news/`, `/new-packaging/`, `/new-website/`, `/tradefair2010/`): nur Überschriften „New Product“, „New Website“, „Trade Fair 2010“; „New Website“: „Today Gozo Cottage has launched a new web portal […]“ (2010). Kein verwertbarer Inhalt.
